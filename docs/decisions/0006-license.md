# 0006. License: GPL-3.0

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-08

## Context

The repository was initialised with the GPL-3.0 text.

## Decision

Keep GPL-3.0 for original work here (tools, scripts, documentation, and original annotations). Do not change without a new ADR.

## Consequences

- Derivative tools stay open.
- The license covers only what the contributors own. It grants no rights in the game, whose copyright belongs to its owners; the README says so.
- Contributors agree their contributions are under GPL-3.0 by submitting them.
